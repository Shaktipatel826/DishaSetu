Why Direct Trade Matters

We are transforming India's agricultural supply chain for fair trade and pure nutrition.

❌

Old Middleman System

Multi-tier brokers & mandi agents
  • ⚠️ Farmers get underpaid (often 40-50% below real market value).
  • ⚠️ Customers pay inflated rates due to commission markups.
  • ⚠️ Chemical polishing & adulteration during storage.
  • ⚠️ Zero traceability of where food was cultivated.
✅

Kisan Direct Model

Direct farmer-to-consumer marketplace
  • ✨ Farmers earn 100% of the listed grain price directly.
  • ✨ Customers save up to 30% compared to supermarket brands.
  • ✨ Fresh, unpolished & single-origin harvest quality.
  • ✨ Direct connection: Know the farmer, village, and harvest date.
